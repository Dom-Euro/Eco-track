Benchmark note

Victorian Department of Education benchmarks are reference figures for utility use, not guaranteed averages for every school. A valid comparison requires matching dates, the relevant student enrolment, and compatible units.

  • Secondary-school electricity benchmark: 400 kWh per student per year.
  • Mains water benchmark: 4 kL per student per year.
  • Waste to landfill benchmark: 0.3 m³ per student per year.

About the project

About EcoTrack

Purpose

EcoTrack helps schools organise and understand their sustainability data in one simple place. It provides a clear way to record, review and compare electricity, water and waste information.

Intended users

Students, teachers, and sustainability coordinators can use the site to collect and interpret resource-use data.

Main features

  • Sustainability dashboard
  • Data entry and record management
  • Charts and analysis
  • Benchmark comparison guidance
  • Goal tracking
  • CSV import and export

Limitations

EcoTrack does not automatically connect to school meters. Data must be entered manually or imported. Browser localStorage is stored in the current device and is not a shared school database. Data quality affects the reliability of conclusions, and benchmarks are reference figures rather than exact school averages.

Sustainability connection

Monitoring consumption and waste supports responsible resource use, improved awareness and better decision-making. This connects with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals for responsible consumption and climate action.

  • SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production
  • SDG 13: Climate Action

Privacy and security

This prototype does not collect student names, passwords or sensitive information. It does not provide a real authentication system and stores data in browser localStorage only. A production system would require server-side authentication, secure data storage, backups and access control.
